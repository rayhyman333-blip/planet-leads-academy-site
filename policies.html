Policies — Planet Leads Academy 🚀

Enrollment & Payment

Pay in full upfront for a course. No pay-later, no installments, no subscription for Academy courses in this catalog. The exact final total is shown on the Gumroad course page before you pay. Planet Leads does not store your card — payment is handled by Gumroad.

Access

After payment, you receive the course through Gumroad. Study at your own pace. Text-based courses have no video, as stated in their course descriptions.

Refunds

Refund terms: 14 days from purchase, full refund, same rule for Gumroad, Shopify Shopper, and Shopify Boutique Dainty. See the full Refund Terms and Rules. Requests go through the purchase record for that course/bundle, with owner review. Email support at planetleads1@gmail.com with course/bundle name, receipt/order id, buyer email, purchase date, amount, and reason.

Tax

Tax, if any, is shown in the final total on the Gumroad course page before you pay. No tax amount is invented here.

Privacy

This local school page does not collect or store your name, email, or payment details on a Planet Leads server. My Courses progress in this local version is saved in your own browser only.

Not Accredited

Planet Leads Academy is not accredited, and does not award degrees or diplomas. Certificates of Completion in this school certify course completion only, not accreditation.
