Frequently Asked Questions

Is Planet Leads Academy accredited?

No. Planet Leads Academy is not accredited and does not award degrees or diplomas. Certificates of Completion certify course completion only.

How do I enroll?

Browse the catalog, open a course page, tap its Gumroad link, and pay in full upfront on Gumroad. No pay-later, no installments, no subscription for Academy courses in this catalog.

Are courses video or text?

Text-based, as stated in each course description. No video is claimed for these courses.

How do I track progress and get a certificate?

Use My Courses (Student System v2): add a course you bought, tick off its 8 lessons, and when all 8 are done, generate your printable Certificate of Completion. Progress is saved in this browser only until the server system is live.

What if a price I see does not match?

School and Shopify prices are $49.99 / $59.99. Gumroad checkout is being updated to match. If a price does not match, email the owner at rayhyman333@gmail.com before you buy, or ask Talk to Cynthia.

What is Cynthia in this school?

Starter Cynthia — Cynthia OS, by Planet Leads, created by Ramel Hyman — tutor, guide, and help desk in plain English, with owner review and admin manual override on technical issues. No new AI subscription and no API key are required. The Enterprise Muse-like version is for big businesses only and is not claimed live here.

Do you offer bundles?

Yes — Professional Bundle (3 courses: Leadership Mastery, Python, Conversational Spanish) $129.99, and Enterprise Bundle (10 courses) $299.99, both on Gumroad with exact course lists on the Bundles page. Singles are $49.99/$59.99, pay in full upfront.
