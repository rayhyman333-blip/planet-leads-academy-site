How enrollment works

1. Browse the catalog. 2. Open a course page. 3. Tap its Gumroad link. 4. Pay in full upfront on Gumroad — no pay-later, no installments. 5. Study the written lessons at your own pace. 6. Come back to Talk to Cynthia for help, or email school support (Ramel Hyman) at planetleads1@gmail.com.

Boutique Academy listings are also verified at the same $49–$59.99 prices (9 Academy courses, all Active). Contact the owner at rayhyman333@gmail.com if a price you see does not match. Help suggestions — try these first

Can't find your course? Go to Catalog, search the exact course name, open its course page, then tap its Gumroad link. Every course page links to the real Gumroad course — no dead ends.

Paid but can't open the course? Check your Gumroad receipt email first (course name, buyer email, amount $49–$59.99). Then email support at planetleads1@gmail.com with the course name + Gumroad receipt/order id + buyer email, and the owner will verify it against the Gumroad purchase record.

Price doesn't match? School, Gumroad (all 35 courses), Shopper (9 courses), and Boutique (9 courses) are all verified at $49–$59.99. Tell support the course name, where you saw the different price, and the price you saw — do not pay a different amount until support confirms it.

Progress or certificate won't save? Progress and certificates work in this browser now (Student System v2), and also on the live Student Login server system, where progress follows you across devices after owner verification. If you use browser-only progress and switch phone/computer, progress in the old browser will not follow you — use Student Login for cross-device progress, or email support.

Lesson won't open / page looks wrong? Reload the page once, then try the Catalog link again. If it still fails, email support with the course name, lesson number, and what you see — the owner reviews technical issues and manual override wins on tech.

Want a refund? Refund requests go through the Gumroad purchase record for that course, with owner review. Refund terms: 14 days from purchase, full refund, same rule everywhere (Gumroad, Shopper, Boutique). Email support at planetleads1@gmail.com with the course name + Gumroad receipt/order id + reason, and the owner will review it.

Not sure which course to pick? Talk to Cynthia in plain English — tell her what you want to learn, and she will guide you to the right course page. She does not decide refunds, prices, or technical fixes alone — those go to owner review.

Need help with something else? For all support and questions, email planetleads1@gmail.com. This email is for school help, administration, and questions. Include the course name, what you were trying to do, and what happened — that helps support answer faster.
