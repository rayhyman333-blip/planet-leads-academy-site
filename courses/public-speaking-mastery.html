What You Will Learn

  • You will learn what nerves actually are and how to work with them instead of fighting them
  • You will learn to structure a talk so listeners can follow it without effort: one clear message, a beginning, a middle, and an ending that lands
  • You will learn to open strong in the first minute, so the room pays attention
  • You will learn to use your voice — pace, pauses, volume, and tone — and your body — posture, gestures, and eye contact — to support your words instead of distracting from them
  • You will learn to design slides that help the audience and never replace you as the speaker
  • You will learn to handle questions, including hard ones and ones you cannot answer
  • You will learn to tell short, honest stories that make ideas stick
  • Finally, you will build a practice and feedback routine that improves every talk you give

Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites. You need to be able to read and write in plain language, and to have — or to invent — a topic you can speak about for five to ten minutes. No speaking experience, performance background, or special equipment is required. A phone or computer that can record sound is useful for the practice exercises, so you can listen back to yourself, but it is not essential — a trusted friend listening live works just as well. If you can hold a conversation with one person, you already have the raw material; this course teaches you to hold a conversation with a room.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
