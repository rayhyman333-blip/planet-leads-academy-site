What You Will Learn

  • You will learn the Hebrew alphabet: the twenty-two letters, their names, their sounds, and the final forms of five letters
  • You will learn how Hebrew reading works: direction, syllables, vowel marks, and the difference between consonantal text and pointed text
  • You will learn essential vocabulary, including common words, key theological and narrative terms, and numbers, and you will learn how Hebrew builds words from three-letter roots
  • You will learn the structure of the Tanakh: its three parts, Torah, Neviim, and Ketuvim, and the books in each part
  • You will learn the major stories and themes of the text as literary and historical subjects
  • You will learn the historical and cultural context of the ancient Near East
  • You will learn methods for close reading, including attention to plain meaning, word choice, repetition, and structure
  • Finally, you will learn how to build a sustainable personal study plan and where to continue learning

Prerequisites

You need no background in religious studies, no knowledge of Hebrew, and no prior familiarity with the Hebrew Bible. The course teaches every term it uses, gives Hebrew words in transliteration alongside translation, and explains all context from zero. You need basic reading comfort with long-form text and a willingness to engage thoughtfully with an ancient document and a new alphabet. No particular beliefs are assumed or required; readers from any tradition or with no religious affiliation are equally welcome, and nothing in the course asks you to adopt any belief. Everything else, vocabulary, reading methods, and historical background, is taught inside the lessons. If you can read carefully and follow written instructions, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
