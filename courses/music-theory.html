What You Will Learn

  • You will learn the twelve notes of Western music and how to read them on the treble and bass staffs, including sharps, flats, and ledger lines
  • You will learn rhythm: note values from whole notes to sixteenths, rests, dots, ties, and the common time signatures
  • You will learn to spell major and natural minor scales in any key and to read key signatures
  • You will learn intervals — the distance between two notes — by number and quality, and why some sound smooth and others tense
  • You will learn to build triads and seventh chords and to label them with Roman numerals
  • You will learn the great chord progressions behind countless songs, including I–IV–V and the twelve-bar blues
  • You will learn to read a simple melody note by note and to train your ear with call-and-response exercises
  • Finally, you will compose an original eight-bar melody and know exactly where to go next

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites. This course starts from zero and assumes you cannot read music yet. Everything — even which note is which on the staff — is explained from scratch. You do not need to own an instrument, though having access to any keyboard (a real piano, an inexpensive electronic keyboard, or a keyboard layout in free music software) will make the exercises much more rewarding, because you can hear every example. If you can follow written instructions and are willing to sing a little, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
