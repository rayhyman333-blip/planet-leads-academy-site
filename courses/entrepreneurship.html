Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites — no business degree, no startup experience, and no existing idea required. Everything is explained from zero, including terms like MVP, break-even, and conversion. You do need a willingness to talk to real people (Lesson 1 requires actual conversations, not just reading) and a notebook or document where your exercises accumulate into your business plan. A small budget for testing (even pocket money) helps in Lessons 3 and 4, but every exercise includes a zero-cost version.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
