What You Will Learn

  • You will learn the practical difference between leading and managing, and how to spend your time on both without confusing them
  • You will learn to communicate decisions and expectations clearly, kindly, and in writing when it matters
  • You will learn how roles, trust, and conflict actually function inside teams, and how to intervene when a team goes quiet or fractious
  • You will learn three decision frameworks — reversibility thinking, the 10-10-10 lens, and structured pros-and-cons analysis — and when each one applies
  • You will learn to give feedback that is specific, timely, and actionable, and to receive criticism without defensiveness
  • You will learn a delegation method that keeps accountability clear while giving people real ownership
  • Finally, you will learn how to handle difficult conversations and lead through change, and you will convert all of this into a personal ninety-day plan

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  1. Lesson 1: Leadership vs. management — what actually changes when you lead
  2. Lesson 2: Communication — being clear, direct, and kind
  3. Lesson 3: Team dynamics — roles, trust, and healthy conflict
  4. Lesson 4: Decision frameworks — reversibility, 10-10-10, pros/cons done right
  5. Lesson 5: Feedback — giving it well and receiving it without defensiveness
  6. Lesson 6: Delegation — handing off work without micromanaging
  7. Lesson 7: Difficult conversations and leading through change
  8. Lesson 8: Your personal leadership plan — habits and 90-day goals

Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites. Helpful but not required: some experience working in a team, and at least one situation where you currently influence other people's work (a project you run, a team you supervise, a group you organize). All frameworks are explained from first principles, so readers new to management literature will follow along. You will need a notebook or a blank document for the exercises, and optionally the two worksheet materials included in this package, which you can print or copy.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
