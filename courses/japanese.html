What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will be able to: pronounce Japanese clearly using the five vowels, the special sounds, and the long-vowel and small-っ rules; read and write hiragana and katakana and recognize common kanji; greet people, introduce yourself, and use survival phrases when you do not understand; build sentences with the です/ます pattern, particles, and present-tense verbs; count fluently, tell the time, and use days, months, and dates; ask and answer questions about directions, prices, and everyday topics; talk about things you did using the past tense; make plans and invitations and hold a full restaurant or travel conversation; and run a simple daily practice routine that keeps your speaking improving

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites. This course starts from zero and assumes you have never studied Japanese before. Everything — even how to make each vowel sound and how to read your first hiragana character — is explained from scratch. If you already know a little Japanese, Lesson 1's pronunciation rules are still worth reading, because correcting a bad accent habit early saves months later. You do not need any textbook or prior grammar knowledge; a notebook for writing out the practice exercises is the only thing that will help.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
