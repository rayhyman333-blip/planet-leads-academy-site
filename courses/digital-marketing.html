What You Will Learn

  • You will learn the three fundamentals every marketing effort needs: a defined audience, a clear offer, and a message that connects the two
  • You will learn content marketing — how useful articles, guides, and posts earn attention over time
  • You will learn social media marketing basics: choosing the right platforms, posting consistently, and engaging genuinely
  • You will learn email marketing: building a list honestly, writing emails people open, and automating welcome sequences
  • You will learn how search engines work and the core practices that help the right pages get found
  • You will learn paid advertising basics: how ad auctions work, how to set a budget, and how to write an ad that earns the click
  • You will learn to measure results with the metrics that actually matter — and to ignore the ones that flatter without informing
  • Finally, you will build a complete 90-day marketing plan and get a roadmap for continuing to learn

Prerequisites

You need a computer with internet access and basic comfort using websites, email, and documents — creating an account, writing a document, saving a file. No marketing experience, design skill, or programming is required. You do not need existing social media accounts or an email list; the course teaches you to build from zero. A product or idea to practice with is strongly recommended but not required — you may invent one for the exercises. Everything else — vocabulary, techniques, and habits — is taught inside the lessons. If you can write a clear paragraph and follow written instructions, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
