What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will: hold drumsticks with a relaxed matched grip and sit at the kit with balanced posture; read simple drum notation for the snare, bass drum, hi-hat, and crash; play the basic rock beat steadily at a slow tempo; vary hi-hat patterns with open and closed strokes and eighth-note and quarter-note feels; play snare patterns with backbeats, rim clicks, and ghost notes; control the bass drum with heel-down and heel-up technique and clean double strokes; build one-beat, two-beat, and one-measure fills that resolve on beat one; play along to songs by counting, listening for the form, and locking in with the bass line; and follow a permanent thirty-minute practice routine with a clear map of what to learn next

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  1. Lesson 1: Grip, Posture, and Setting Up at the Kit
  2. Lesson 2: Reading Simple Drum Notation
  3. Lesson 3: The Basic Rock Beat
  4. Lesson 4: Hi-Hat and Snare Patterns
  5. Lesson 5: Bass Drum Control
  6. Lesson 6: Fills - One-Beat, Two-Beat, and Full-Measure Fills
  7. Lesson 7: Playing Along to Songs
  8. Lesson 8: Your Practice System + What to Learn Next

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites beyond access to an instrument. Any drum kit, electronic kit, or practice pad with a pair of drumsticks will work for this course; a full acoustic kit is ideal but not required, and the early lessons can be done entirely on a pad. You need no prior musical experience, no ability to read music, and no theory background. Everything is taught from zero, one step at a time.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
