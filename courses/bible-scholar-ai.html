What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will be able to: (1) describe the main study approaches used by careful Bible readers, including observation, interpretation, and application; (2) summarize the storyline of the Old Testament from Genesis through Malachi and of the New Testament from the Gospels through Revelation; (3) explain key themes the text itself emphasizes, such as covenant, grace, and the kingdom; (4) adjust your reading strategy for narrative, poetry, letters, and prophecy; (5) establish a simple, sustainable prayer and devotional practice; and (6) build a personal reading plan with schedules you can actually finish
  • Each lesson also names common misreading pitfalls so you learn not just what to do but what to watch out for

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  2. Lesson 2: Old Testament Overview: The Storyline from Genesis to Malachi

  4. Lesson 4: Big Themes: Covenant, Grace, and the Kingdom
  5. Lesson 5: Jesus: His Life, Parables, and Core Teachings
  6. Lesson 6: Reading by Genre: Narrative, Poetry, Letters, Prophecy
  7. Lesson 7: Prayer and Devotional Practice
  8. Lesson 8: Your Study Plan: Reading Schedules and Next Steps

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites. You do not need prior Bible knowledge, a theology background, or any particular church affiliation. Access to a Bible in a translation you can understand is recommended — the lessons quote briefly from the text, but having the full context in front of you makes the exercises far richer. A notebook or printed copies of the included worksheets are all you need besides a willingness to read carefully and reflect honestly.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
