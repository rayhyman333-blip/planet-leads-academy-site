What You Will Learn

  • You will learn how Muslims approach the study of their religion: the Quran and the Sunnah as primary sources, and the student's etiquette of sincerity, humility, and seeking qualified teachers
  • You will learn the six articles of faith that form Islamic theology
  • You will learn the structure of the Quran, its major themes, and methods for reading it with comprehension
  • You will learn the key periods of the Prophet's life and the lessons Muslims draw from them
  • You will learn the five pillars of Islam and how they function in daily practice, with practical guides to prayer and fasting
  • You will learn the Islamic framework of ethics — character, family, and community responsibilities
  • Finally, you will build a personal, lifelong study routine suited to your schedule and level

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  1. Lesson 1: How to study Islam — the Quran and Sunnah as sources, a student's approach
  2. Lesson 2: Aqidah — the six articles of faith explained
  3. Lesson 3: The Quran — structure, major themes, reading with understanding
  4. Lesson 4: The Seerah — key periods of the Prophet's life and their lessons
  5. Lesson 5: Fiqh essentials — the five pillars in practice
  6. Lesson 6: Daily worship made practical — prayer and fasting guides
  7. Lesson 7: Islamic ethics — character, family, and community
  8. Lesson 8: Your study path — building a lifelong learning routine

Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites and no assumed background in Arabic or Islamic studies. All Arabic terms are introduced with plain explanations, and all concepts are built from first principles. You will need a notebook or document for the exercises, a copy of the Quran in a translation you can read comfortably (any reputable translation is fine for this course's purposes), and optionally the study-tracker material included in this package.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
