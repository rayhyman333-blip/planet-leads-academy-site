What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will: breathe with low, supported breaths and stand with balanced, relaxed posture; find your comfortable vocal range and know your lowest and highest easy notes; match pitch accurately and hear intervals by ear; warm up safely with gentle slides, hums, and lip trills; shape pure vowels and pronounce consonants clearly without tightening the jaw; sing a simple song with steady phrasing, clear words, and a planned breath map; sing a harmony part above and below a melody and hold your line against another voice; and follow a permanent twenty-minute daily practice routine with a clear map of what to learn next

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites beyond a willingness to sing. You need no prior musical experience, no ability to read music, and no theory background. A piano, keyboard, or tuning app is helpful for finding pitches, and any simple instrument or app that plays single notes will do. Everything is taught from zero, one step at a time, and every exercise is designed to be sung gently, within a comfortable range.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
