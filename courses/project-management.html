What You Will Learn

  • You will learn what project management is and the five phases every project moves through: starting, planning, doing, monitoring, and closing
  • You will learn to define scope — writing down exactly what the project includes and excludes — and to set goals that can actually be measured
  • You will learn to break work into small tasks, sequence them, estimate honestly, and build a schedule you can trust
  • You will learn budgeting basics: listing costs, adding contingency, and tracking spending against the plan
  • You will learn to lead a team without authority: clarifying roles, running useful meetings, and communicating so nobody is surprised
  • You will learn risk management: identifying what could go wrong, judging likelihood and impact, and preparing responses in advance
  • You will learn to track progress with simple status checks and to handle change requests without derailing the project
  • Finally, you will plan a complete sample project in the capstone and get a roadmap for continuing to learn

Prerequisites

You need no prior management experience, business training, or special software — a notebook or any document app is enough for every exercise in this course. Basic comfort with everyday arithmetic (adding costs, comparing dates) is all the math involved. A real project of your own to practice on is strongly recommended but not required; you may use the course's fundraiser example throughout if you prefer. Everything else — vocabulary, frameworks, and habits — is taught inside the lessons in plain language. If you can organize a to-do list and follow written instructions, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
