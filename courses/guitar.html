What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will: hold the guitar in a stable, comfortable position; tune all six strings accurately; read chord diagrams fluently; play the essential open chords (E minor, A major, G, C, D, A minor) with every string ringing clean; strum three core patterns in steady time with a metronome; switch between chords without stopping your strumming hand; play the A minor pentatonic scale with alternate picking and improvise simple phrases; perform finger-strengthening exercises and form your first barre chords; and follow a permanent thirty-minute practice routine with a clear list of songs to learn next

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  1. Lesson 1: Holding the Guitar, Tuning Up, and Your First Notes
  2. Lesson 2: Reading Chord Diagrams - Your First Chords (E Minor, A Major)
  3. Lesson 3: Strumming - Patterns, Rhythm, and Timing
  4. Lesson 4: The Essential Open Chords (G, C, D, E Minor, A Minor)
  5. Lesson 5: Switching Chords Smoothly + Your First Full Song
  6. Lesson 6: Single Notes - The Minor Pentatonic Scale
  7. Lesson 7: Finger Exercises and an Intro to Barre Chords
  8. Lesson 8: Your Practice Routine + Five Songs to Learn Next

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites beyond access to an instrument. Any six-string acoustic or electric guitar in playable condition will work - the lessons assume a standard-tuned right-handed guitar, and left-handed players can mirror the instructions. You will also want an electronic clip-on tuner and a pick, both inexpensive. You need no prior musical experience, no ability to read music, and no theory background. Everything is taught from zero, one step at a time.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
