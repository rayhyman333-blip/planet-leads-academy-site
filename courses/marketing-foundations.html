What You Will Learn

  • You will learn what marketing really is and how it differs from selling and advertising
  • You will learn how to describe your customer in concrete detail, including their situation, their problems, and what they want
  • You will learn positioning — how to explain what makes your offer different in a way that matters to the customer — and how to turn that into a clear message
  • You will learn how to create content that earns attention by being useful
  • You will learn email basics: building a list with permission, writing emails people open, and sending consistently
  • You will learn social media basics: choosing where to show up, posting sustainably, and engaging genuinely
  • You will learn to measure what works using a small set of numbers you can actually track
  • Finally, you will assemble everything into a simple 30-day marketing plan that fits the hours you really have

Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites. You need a computer or phone with internet access and basic comfort using websites, email, and documents — writing a document, saving a file, sending an email. No marketing experience, design skill, or programming is required. You do not need existing social media accounts, an email list, or a website; the course teaches you to start from zero. A product or idea to practise with is strongly recommended but not required — you may invent one for the exercises. Everything else — vocabulary, techniques, and habits — is taught inside the lessons. If you can write a clear paragraph and follow written instructions, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
