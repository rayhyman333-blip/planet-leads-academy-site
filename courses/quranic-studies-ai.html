What You Will Learn

  • You will learn the Arabic alphabet: the twenty-eight letters, their names, their sounds, and their four forms depending on position in a word
  • You will learn how Arabic reading works: direction, joining, vowel marks, and the difference between Quranic script and everyday printed Arabic
  • You will learn essential vocabulary, including common words, key terms of the text, and numbers, and you will learn how Arabic builds words from three-letter roots
  • You will learn the structure of the Quran: its 114 surahs, their division into verses, the thirty-part reading division, and the distinction between Meccan and Medinan surahs
  • You will learn the major themes and passages of the text as literary and historical subjects
  • You will learn the historical context of seventh-century Arabia and the centuries of scholarship that followed
  • You will learn methods for close reading, including attention to context, language, and structure
  • Finally, you will learn how to build a sustainable personal study plan and where to continue learning

Prerequisites

You need no background in religious studies, no knowledge of Arabic, and no prior familiarity with the Quran. The course teaches every term it uses, gives Arabic words in transliteration alongside translation, and explains all context from zero. You need basic reading comfort with long-form text and a willingness to engage thoughtfully with an ancient document and a new alphabet. No particular beliefs are assumed or required; readers from any tradition or with no religious affiliation are equally welcome, and nothing in the course asks you to adopt any belief. Everything else, vocabulary, reading methods, and historical background, is taught inside the lessons. If you can read carefully and follow written instructions, you are ready.

Estimated study time: 6 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.
