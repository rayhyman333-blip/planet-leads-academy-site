What You Will Learn

  • By the end of this course you will: sit with healthy posture and a correct, relaxed hand position; find any note on the keyboard using the black-key pattern; read the treble clef, note values, and rhythm in 4/4 time; play five-finger patterns and the one-octave C major scale with correct fingering; build major and minor triads and play the C-G-Am-F progression; read the bass clef and play root, root-fifth, and broken-chord accompaniment patterns with the left hand; combine both hands using a reliable section-by-section method; and shape music with dynamics, tempo markings, and phrasing
  • You will finish with a permanent practice system and a clear map of what to learn next

Curriculum — 8 lessons

  1. Lesson 1: Posture, Hand Position, and Finding Your Way Around the Keyboard
  2. Lesson 2: Reading Music - The Staff, Treble Clef, Note Values and Rhythm
  3. Lesson 3: Five-Finger Patterns and Your First Scale (C Major)
  4. Lesson 4: Chords - Building Triads, the C-G-Am-F Progression
  5. Lesson 5: The Left Hand - Bass Notes and Simple Accompaniment Patterns
  6. Lesson 6: Hands Together - Your First Complete Piece, Step by Step
  7. Lesson 7: Dynamics, Tempo, and Playing with Expression
  8. Lesson 8: Your Practice System + Next Pieces to Learn

Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites beyond access to an instrument. Any piano or touch-sensitive keyboard with at least five octaves will work for this course; a full 88-key instrument is ideal but not required. You need no prior musical experience, no ability to read music, and no theory background. Everything is taught from zero, one step at a time.

Estimated study time: 8 hours, at your own pace, no deadline. This is an estimate for reading the 8 lessons and doing the practice exercises — not a verified average completion time, and not a course length guarantee.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to this course through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
