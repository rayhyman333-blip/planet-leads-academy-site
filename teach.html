What you get

  • A 90/10 split in your favor on your own courses' net revenue.
  • Our course-building tools — your curriculum is packaged in the Academy's content shell with a syllabus, an 8-lesson structure, and student materials.
  • Distribution — your course listed across the Academy's sales channels, starting with Gumroad and Payhip.
  • A real student system — buyers get access by code on purchase, track their progress, and earn a certificate at 8/8 lessons.
  • Cynthia — the Academy's assistant answers buyer and student questions about your course from verified facts, around the clock.
  • Your content stays yours — you keep ownership; the Academy licenses it to sell while you teach with us. Either side can step away on 30 days' notice, and your students keep the courses they bought.

What you bring

  • Your subject, and the experience to teach it.
  • A full curriculum plan (module + lesson outline).
  • One complete sample lesson.
  • Lessons that are your own original work.
