Privacy Policy — Planet Leads Academy 🚀

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Planet Leads Academy ("the Academy", "we") is an online school founded by Ramel Hyman, operated through Planet Leads. This policy covers the Planet Leads Academy Android app and the Academy website it opens.

What the app is

The Android app is a wrapper around the Academy website (rayhyman333-blip.github.io/planet-leads-academy-site). The app itself:

  • collects no personal data;
  • requests no Android permissions;
  • contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • stores nothing on your device beyond the normal browser (Chrome) cache and site data that any website visit creates, which you can clear in Chrome or Android settings at any time.

The website and student accounts

Studying a course requires a student account on the Academy website. When you register and sign in on the website, the information you provide (such as your name and email address and your lesson progress) is processed by the website's student system so you can sign in, get verified, and track your 8 lessons per course across devices. Sign-in happens on the website, inside the app's browser view — the app never sees or stores your password.

The "Talk to Cynthia" chat on the website keeps its history only in your own browser, and only if you tick the box asking it to. It sends nothing anywhere in its verified-answer mode.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. Courses are bought on our website and web storefronts (such as Gumroad). Payment is handled by those storefronts and their payment processors under their own privacy policies. Planet Leads does not receive or store your card number.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising.

Account and data deletion

You can ask for your student account and its data to be deleted at any time. Email planetleads1@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for deletion; the Academy will delete your account and its progress records and confirm by email when it is done.

Children

The Academy's courses are written for adult learners. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach the Academy through the contact routes on the Academy website, or email planetleads1@gmail.com.
