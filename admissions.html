Admissions — How to Enroll

Anyone who can read the written lessons in English, use a web browser, and pay in full upfront on Gumroad can enroll. No prior degree, diploma, or qualification is required for any course. No entrance test, interview, or portfolio is required.

How to get started, step by step

  1. Browse. Open the Catalog. Pick a course or a bundle.
  2. Check the price. Singles are $49–$59.99. Professional Bundle is $129.99. Enterprise Bundle is $299.99. The final total is shown on Gumroad before you pay.
  3. Buy in full upfront. Follow the course’s Gumroad link and pay in full. There is no pay-later, installment, or subscription option for Academy courses.
  4. Register on Student Login. Go to Student Login. Register with a name and email, and sign in.
  5. Submit your purchase for owner verification. On Student Login, submit your Gumroad purchase (course, receipt id, buyer email, amount, purchase date) for owner verification.
  6. Change your temporary password. When the owner verifies your enrollment, you will sign in with a temporary password issued on verification. You must change it on first sign-in before any progress is recorded. It expires after your first password change, or after 72 hours, whichever is first.
  7. Study. Work through lessons 1–8 at your own pace, with a practice exercise in each lesson. There is no deadline.
  8. Certificate. At 8/8 on a verified enrollment, get your printable Certificate of Completion for that course. It is not accredited and not a degree.

Registration, verification, and progress

  • Registration is on the school’s Student Login page, not by email alone. Email alone does not create a student account.
  • Owner verification is manual: the owner checks the Gumroad purchase record. There is no automatic purchase verification claimed.
  • Progress is tracked per course, lesson by lesson, in Student Login, across devices, after verification.
  • If progress is blocked, check first: are you signed in, is the enrollment verified, and have you changed a temporary password if one was issued? Progress stays blocked while a temporary password change is still required.
  • Forgot your password? Use the Student Login recovery route, or a backup code shown once at registration. Planet Leads does not store your card, and it never asks you to send a password or card number in the guided chat box.

Qualifications, deadlines, and transfers

  • No qualification is required to start, and no qualification is awarded at the end except the Certificate of Completion described above.
  • No deadline, no term dates, and no transfer of progress from another school is offered.
  • If a subject needs a tool — a guitar, a piano/keyboard, or a computer for programming — that is a practical need for studying that subject, not an admission test.
  • If you buy the wrong course, use the 14-day full refund rule (see Tuition & Fees) and email planetleads1@gmail.com with your receipt details.
