Ask the Course Tutor

The course tutor answers questions about the material in the Academy's 35 courses. It works the same way as Cynthia's tutor on the Academy's MCP server: it finds the lesson your question belongs to and answers with an exact excerpt from that lesson, with the course and lesson cited. It never paraphrases, never uses outside knowledge, and when no lesson covers your question it says so honestly instead of guessing.

Everything runs in your browser on this page — your question is not sent anywhere. The tutor's library is the full text of all 35 courses (280 lessons), the same text students receive in their course packages. For account, enrollment, or refund questions, see the Help page or Talk to Cynthia.
