Privacy Policy — Cynthia, by Planet Leads

Publisher: Planet Leads (sole proprietor: Ramel Hyman) · Contact: planetleads1@gmail.com
Effective date: November 19, 2026

Cynthia is a verified-answer assistant. She answers questions about Planet Leads, Planet Leads Academy, and Planet Leads' services from an owner-approved store of verified facts, and runs as a plugin connected to a public MCP server operated by Planet Leads. This policy explains exactly what data that involves. It is short because Cynthia collects very little.

What Cynthia receives

  • Your questions. When you use the plugin, the question text you ask Cynthia (and, for the course tutor, the course topic you ask about) is sent to Cynthia's server so she can look up the answer. That is the only content the service needs from you.
  • No account, no sign-in. Cynthia requires no account, no login, and no profile. She does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifier.

The question log — disclosed in full

Cynthia's server keeps a working log of questions so the owner can see what people ask and add verified answers for questions she could not answer yet. Each log entry contains only:

  • the question text,
  • which verified fact matched, or that nothing matched,
  • a UTC timestamp.

No IP addresses and no user identifiers are logged. The log is bounded to the most recent 2,000 questions, and it lives on the hosted server's ephemeral storage — it resets whenever the service restarts or is redeployed, so it is a short-lived working view, not a permanent archive.

What Cynthia never collects

  • Payment card data of any kind (Cynthia does not store cards and processes no payments; payments for Planet Leads products are handled by the payment provider on its own pages).
  • Passwords, API keys, or any other credentials or secrets.
  • Government identifiers.
  • Health information.
  • Precise location data.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, ID numbers, or health information in questions you ask Cynthia — she does not need them and cannot use them.

Purposes

Question text is used for two purposes only: (1) to look up and return your answer, and (2) in the aggregate question log described above, so the owner can improve the verified fact store. Cynthia does not build profiles, does not track you across services, and does not use your questions for advertising.

Who receives data

Your questions go to Cynthia's server, which is operated by Planet Leads and hosted on third-party hosting infrastructure that processes requests in order to deliver the service. Planet Leads does not sell personal data, and does not share the question log with third parties for their own purposes.

Retention

  • Answers are computed at request time.
  • The question log retains at most the most recent 2,000 entries and is wiped on service restart or redeployment, as described above. There is no long-term archive of questions.

Your controls

  • You choose what to put in a question. Asking Cynthia never requires personal information.
  • If you believe a question you asked contains personal information you want removed, contact planetleads1@gmail.com. Because the log holds no identifiers, tell us the question text and approximately when you asked it, and we will remove the matching entry.

Children

Cynthia provides general-audience business and course information. She is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.
