This lesson teaches you to talk about the future — making plans, expressing wants, inviting people — and handles two of the most useful real-world conversations: ordering in a restaurant and navigating travel. Future talk turns you from a visitor into someone who can actually live in Mandarin: booking, inviting, promising, and planning.

Mandarin marks the future with helper words before the verb, since verbs never change form. 要 (yào) means "want to / will": 我明天要去商店 (wǒ míng tiān yào qù shāng diàn) — I will go to the store tomorrow. 会 (huì) means "will" with a sense of likelihood or learned ability: 明天会下雨 (míng tiān huì xià yǔ) — it will rain tomorrow. 我会说中文 (wǒ huì shuō Zhōng wén) — I can speak Chinese. 想 (xiǎng) means "would like to": 我想去中国 (wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōng guó) — I'd like to go to China. Future time words: 明天 (tomorrow), 后天 (the day after tomorrow), 下个星期 (next week), 下个月 (next month), 明年 (next year), 晚上 (this evening). Combine freely: 我下个星期要去北京 (wǒ xià gè xīng qī yào qù Běi jīng) — I'm going to Beijing next week.

Invitations are simple and warm. ...好吗? (...hǎo ma? — is ... okay?): 我们一起去吃饭，好吗? (wǒ men yì qǐ qù chī fàn, hǎo ma? — let's go eat together, okay?). 一起 (yì qǐ) means "together" and appears in most invitations. Accept with 好啊! (hǎo a! — great!) or 太好了! (tài hǎo le! — wonderful!). Decline softly with 下次吧 (xià cì ba — next time) or 我明天有事 (wǒ míng tiān yǒu shì — I have something tomorrow).

Restaurant phrases. The server may greet you with 几位? (jǐ wèi? — how many people?). Ask for the menu: 菜单 (cài dān) — 服务员，菜单! (fú wù yuán, cài dān! — waiter, the menu!). Ask for a recommendation: 你有什么推荐? (nǐ yǒu shén me tuī jiàn?). Order with 我要... (wǒ yào... — I want...): 我要这个 (wǒ yào zhè ge) — I want this one. React to food: 太好吃了! (tài hǎo chī le! — so delicious!). Dietary needs: 不要辣 (bú yào là) — not spicy. 少放盐 (shǎo fàng yán) — less salt. Ask for the bill: 买单! (mǎi dān! — the check!). Common mistake: snapping fingers or shouting to get attention — a raised hand with 服务员 (fú wù yuán) is polite and effective.

Travel phrases. 机场 (jī chǎng, airport), 飞机 (fēi jī, airplane), 酒店 (jiǔ diàn, hotel), 火车 (huǒ chē, train), 票 (piào, ticket), 预订 (yù dìng, reservation). At the hotel: 我有预订 (wǒ yǒu yù dìng) — I have a reservation. 我要办入住 (wǒ yào bàn rù zhù) — I'd like to check in. 早餐几点开始? (zǎo cān jǐ diǎn kāi shǐ? — what time does breakfast start?). Problems: 我把护照丢了 (wǒ bǎ hù zhào diū le) — I lost my passport. 请帮帮我 (qǐng bāng bang wǒ) — please help me.

Dialogue 1 — at a restaurant:

Server: 几位? (Jǐ wèi?) — How many?
Bo: 两位。有靠窗的位置吗? (Liǎng wèi. Yǒu kào chuāng de wèi zhi ma?) — Two. Do you have a window seat?
Server: 有的，请跟我来。这是菜单。 (Yǒu de, qǐng gēn wǒ lái. Zhè shì cài dān.) — Yes, follow me please. Here is the menu.
Bo: 你有什么推荐? (Nǐ yǒu shén me tuī jiàn?) — What do you recommend?
Server: 宫保鸡丁很好吃。 (Gōng bǎo jī dīng hěn hǎo chī.) — Kung pao chicken is very tasty.
Bo: 好，我要一个宫保鸡丁，还要两碗米饭。 (Hǎo, wǒ yào yí gè gōng bǎo jī dīng, hái yào liǎng wǎn mǐ fàn.) — Good, I want one kung pao chicken and two bowls of rice.
(later) Bo: 太好吃了! 买单! (Tài hǎo chī le! Mǎi dān!) — So delicious! The check!
Server: 一共八十五块。 (Yí gòng bā shí wǔ kuài.) — 85 yuan altogether.

Dialogue 2 — making weekend plans:

Nan: 你星期六做什么? (Nǐ xīng qī liù zuò shén me?) — What are you doing Saturday?
Ping: 我想去公园。你呢? (Wǒ xiǎng qù gōng yuán. Nǐ ne?) — I'd like to go to the park. And you?
Nan: 我要去买东西。我们一起去，好吗? (Wǒ yào qù mǎi dōng xi. Wǒ men yì qǐ qù, hǎo ma?) — I'm going shopping. Let's go together, okay?
Ping: 好啊! 我们几点见面? (Hǎo a! Wǒ men jǐ diǎn jiàn miàn?) — Great! What time shall we meet?
Nan: 上午九点，在地铁站见。 (Shàng wǔ jiǔ diǎn, zài dì tiě zhàn jiàn.) — 9 AM, meet at the subway station.
Ping: 好的，明天见! (Hǎo de, míng tiān jiàn!) — Okay, see you tomorrow!

Practice: Write three plans for next week using 要 or 会, and three wants using 想. Write a full restaurant dialogue (8+ lines) where you enter, order two dishes and drinks, react to the food, and ask for the bill. Say it all aloud with correct tones. Finally, invite a friend somewhere in Mandarin using 一起...好吗, and answer your own invitation.
