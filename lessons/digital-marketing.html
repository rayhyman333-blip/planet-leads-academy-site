Full Lessons — Digital Marketing

Full lesson text, Academy-built. This is the complete 8-lesson course text for this course. Prices and course links are on the course page.

Digital marketing is not posting on social media and hoping someone buys. It is a system: you find people who already have a problem or desire, you put a clear, helpful message in front of them at the right moment, and you make it easy for them to take the next step. Every channel — search, email, social media, video, paid ads, a website — is just a place where that system can happen. If you understand the system, you can learn any new channel quickly. If you only learn button-clicking on one platform, you will have to start over every time a platform changes.
