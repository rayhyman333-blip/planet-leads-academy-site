Full Lessons — Mobile Development

Full lesson text, Academy-built. This is the complete 8-lesson course text for this course. Prices and course links are on the course page.

A mobile app is software designed to run on a phone or tablet, using the device's screen, touch input, sensors, and connection to the internet. That sounds simple, but it hides important choices. The two major platforms, Apple's iOS and Google's Android, have different design conventions, distribution stores, and technical rules. Users, meanwhile, expect apps to open quickly, work with one hand, respect their battery and data, and keep their information safe. Understanding those expectations before writing code prevents building something technically impressive that nobody enjoys using.
