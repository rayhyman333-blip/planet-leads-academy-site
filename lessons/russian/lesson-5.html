This lesson turns you into someone who can get answers: how to form every kind of question, ask for directions, handle a full shopping conversation, and keep small talk going. Up to now you have mostly made statements; questions are what make you independent in a Russian-speaking place. The good news: Russian questions are simpler than English ones — no "do/does/did" helpers, just intonation or a question word.

The core question words (memorize with an example each): Кто? (kto, who) — Кто это? (who is this?); Что? (shto, what) — Что это? (what is this?); Где? (gdye, where) — Где банк? (where is the bank?); Когда? (kag-DA, when) — Когда встреча? (when is the meeting?); Как? (kak, how) — Как дела? (how are things?); Почему? (pa-chye-MU, why) — Почему нет? (why not?); Сколько? (SKOL-ka, how much/many) — Сколько стоит? (how much is it?); Какой? (ka-KOY, which/what kind of) — Какой автобус? (which bus?). To form a yes/no question, keep normal word order and raise your intonation at the end: Вы говорите по-русски? Ты понимаешь? You can also add ли after the verb for a more formal written style (Говорите ли вы...?), but intonation alone is how people actually speak. Note: что is pronounced "shto", not "chto" — one of the most audible beginner markers, so fix it today.

Directions vocabulary is survival-level. Где находится...? (gdye na-KHO-di-tsa, where is... located?) is more precise than Где...? for buildings. Как пройти к...? (kak pray-TI k, how do I get to...?) — Как пройти к метро? (how do I get to the metro?). Key answers to recognize: направо (na-PRA-va, to the right), налево (na-LYE-va, to the left), прямо (PRYA-ma, straight ahead), рядом (RYA-dam, nearby), далеко (da-lye-KO, far), перекрёсток (pye-rye-KRYO-stak, intersection), угол (U-gal, corner), мост (most, bridge). The lifesaver follow-up: Покажите, пожалуйста, на карте (pa-ka-ZHI-tye, pa-ZHA-luy-sta na KAR-tye — please show me on the map). Polite opener: Извините, где находится музей? (Excuse me, where is the museum?).

Shopping questions beyond prices: У вас есть...? (u vas yest', do you have...?) — У вас есть молоко? Можно посмотреть? (MOZH-na pa-sma-TRYET', may I look?) — essential before touching goods in small shops. Можно примерить? (MOZH-na pri-MYE-rit', can I try it on?) for clothing. У вас есть размер больше/меньше? (raz-MYER BOL-shye/MYEN-shye, do you have a bigger/smaller size?). Вы принимаете карты? (vy pri-ni-MA-i-tye KAR-ty, do you take cards?). Small-talk questions that keep conversations alive: Откуда вы? (at-KU-da vy, where are you from?), Чем вы занимаетесь? (chyem vy za-ni-MA-i-tyes', what do you do for work?), Как долго вы здесь? (how long have you been here?), Вам нравится город? (vam NRA-vi-tsa GO-rat, do you like the city?), Что вы посоветуете? (shto vy pa-sa-VYE-tu-i-tye, what do you recommend?). Two mistakes to avoid: saying "Что ваше имя?" instead of Как вас зовут? (nobody says the first), and answering a question you only half understood — use Я не совсем понимаю, повторите, пожалуйста (I don't quite understand, please repeat) instead of guessing.

Dialogue 1 — asking directions:

— Извините, где находится музей? (gdye na-KHO-di-tsa mu-ZYEY) — Excuse me, where is the museum?
— Музей рядом. Идите прямо, потом направо. (rya-DAM, i-DI-tye PRYA-ma, pa-TOM na-PRA-va) — The museum is nearby. Go straight, then right.
— Это далеко пешком? (da-lye-KO pyesh-KOM) — Is it far on foot?
— Нет, пять минут. Музей между банком и аптекой. (MYEZH-du BAN-kam i ap-TYE-kay) — No, five minutes. The museum is between the bank and the pharmacy.
— Большое спасибо! Как называется эта улица? (bal-SHO-ye spa-SI-ba) — Thank you very much! What is this street called?
— Улица Ленина. (U-li-tsa LYE-ni-na) — Lenin Street.

Dialogue 2 — small talk at a café:

— Откуда вы? (at-KU-da vy) — Where are you from?
— Я из Англии. А вы? (ya iz AN-gli-i) — I am from England. And you?
— Я отсюда, из Москвы. Как долго вы здесь? — I am from here, from Moscow. How long have you been here?
— Две недели. Мне очень нравится город. (dvye nye-DYE-li) — Two weeks. I like the city a lot.
— Что вы посоветуете посмотреть? — What do you recommend seeing?
— Я хочу увидеть парк завтра. Почему бы вам не пойти? (pa-chye-MU by vam nye pay-TI) — I want to see the park tomorrow. Why don't you come?
— Хорошая идея! Во сколько? (kha-RO-sha-ya i-DYE-ya) — Good idea! At what time?
— В десять. Увидимся здесь. (u-VI-dim-sya zdyes') — At ten. See you here.

Practice: Write ten questions — two each for directions, shopping, time, personal info, and opinions — and say each aloud with rising intonation. Then memorize the direction sequence (прямо, направо, налево, на углу) and give directions from your front door to the nearest shop, in Russian, out loud. Finally, role-play Dialogue 2 with a partner or alone, then change three details (city, time, place) and run it again — this is how small talk becomes automatic.
