Student System — Item 7 in the School Plan

What exists now — Student System v2, working in this browser

My Courses now works for all 35 courses: enter your name, add any course you bought on Gumroad, tick off its 8 lessons, and when all 8 are done, generate your Certificate of Completion with your name, course, and date, printable from the certificate page. Progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage, per course). It does not yet log you in, does not remember progress across devices, and does not verify a Gumroad purchase — that is owner-checked against the Gumroad purchase record — those are server features, below.

What the real system must do, before it is claimed live

1. Student login, with password recovery the student controls. 2. Purchase memory: after a Gumroad purchase, the student's courses appear in My Courses on any device. 3. Progress tracking: lesson-by-lesson progress saved on the server, not only in one browser. 4. Certificate issuance: a Certificate of Completion issued only after all 8 lessons of a course are marked complete, clearly stating it is not accredited and not a degree. 5. Owner review: Talk to Cynthia helps, the owner reviews payment, refund, and technical issues, and admin manual override wins on technical issues.

Server version — not live yet

Server login, server purchase record, and cross-device progress are built locally and are not live yet. When deployed, they will be on the Student Login page. Gumroad purchase verification is owner-checked manually against the purchase record — it is not automatic. My Courses in this browser remains available for browser-only progress, with certificates generated from browser progress only.

How to use My Courses now

Open My Courses, pick a course you bought on Gumroad, and mark each lesson complete as you study it. Keep your Gumroad purchase record as your proof of purchase until the server system is live.

Lifetime access: One payment — no subscription, no expiry date, no deadline. You keep access to your courses through the Academy’s student system for as long as Planet Leads Academy operates.
