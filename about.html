What this school is

An online school with 35 courses in Business & Professional, Programming, Music, Languages, and Faith & Spirituality. Each course is 8 written lessons, studied at your own pace, with no deadline and no live timetable.

There is no physical campus, no classrooms, no dormitories, and no physical library building. The school’s facilities are online: this website, the Catalog, course and lesson pages, My Courses / Student Login, the Talk to Cynthia chat box, the certificate page, and email support.

How study works

  1. Browse the Catalog and open a course page.
  2. Follow its Gumroad link and pay in full upfront. The final total is shown on Gumroad before you pay.
  3. Study the 8 written lessons at your own pace, with a practice exercise in each lesson.
  4. Register / sign in on Student Login, and submit your Gumroad purchase for owner verification.
  5. When the owner verifies your enrollment, you sign in with a temporary password issued on verification, and you must change it on first sign-in before progress is recorded. The temporary password expires after your first password change, or after 72 hours, whichever is first.
  6. Track lessons 1–8. At 8/8 on a verified enrollment, get your printable Certificate of Completion.
