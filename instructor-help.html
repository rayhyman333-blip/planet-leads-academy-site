Instructor Help — Planet Leads Academy 🚀

How teaching at Planet Leads Academy works, in one place: applying, the quality gate, how your course gets built, how and when you are paid, how to update your course, and how to reach your students. New here? Start with the Teach at the Academy page.

1. Applying

Email planetleads1@gmail.com with your background, a full curriculum plan, and one complete sample lesson. Accreditation is not required — applications are judged on substance: real teaching or practice experience and a real curriculum. The owner’s decision is final.

2. The quality gate

Every course is reviewed before it is published: the curriculum plan and the full lessons are checked by the Academy first. Acceptance as an instructor is not acceptance of every course — each course passes the gate on its own. After publishing, a course with chronic refunds or substantiated student complaints gets one fix cycle with you, then may be delisted.

3. How your course gets built

You supply the curriculum and lessons; the Academy packages them in its content shell — a syllabus (overview, what students will learn, how to use the course, prerequisites, about the package), 8 lessons, and course materials — and lists the course on the Academy’s sales channels (Gumroad and Payhip today). Students get access through the Academy’s student system, and Cynthia answers student questions from the verified course material. You keep full ownership of your content; the Academy’s license covers its own editions and listings only, and you may teach the same material elsewhere under your own name.

4. How and when you are paid

You keep 90% of the gross sale price of your own courses — 90% of what the student pays, before channel fees; channel fees are the Academy’s cost and are never deducted from your share. Courses sell through the Academy’s storefront accounts, so channels pay the Academy and the Academy pays you. Payouts are monthly: you receive a statement showing each sale, any refunds or offsets, and your payable balance, and payment is made on the 15th of the following month once the balance reaches $25 (smaller balances roll forward; the threshold is waived on a final payout after you exit). Payment is by PayPal by default, Wise for non-US instructors, or US bank transfer on request. A tax form must be on file before your first payout: Form W-9 (US) or Form W-8BEN (non-US). A refunded sale pays out nothing; if a refund or chargeback lands after a payout, that sale’s share is offset against your future payouts — the Academy never claws money back from your account directly. No earnings are promised: the split is a share of whatever your course actually sells.

5. Updating your course

Courses are updated by the Academy from your instructions — send an update request and the Academy edits the course, regenerates it, and re-lists it. Use this template (the button below opens it in your email program, addressed and ready):

Email a course update request →

The template:

Instructor name:
Course:
Lesson(s) affected (numbers or titles, or 'new material'):
What should change:
New text (paste it here, or describe the change clearly):
Anything students should be told (this can become an announcement):

Corrections are made as fast as the Academy can turn them around; a change that affects enrolled students can also be posted as an announcement (section 6).

6. Announcing to your students

Email the announcement text to planetleads1@gmail.com with your course name. It will be posted on the Announcements page and on your course’s page. Announcements live on the site — there is no announcement email or push notification.

7. Ending the arrangement

Either side may end the arrangement on 30 days’ written notice (email counts). Students who already bought your course keep their access. Sales made before the end date are still paid under the usual split and schedule, and any remaining balance is paid in the next payout cycle with the $25 threshold waived.

Questions

For anything else about teaching here, email planetleads1@gmail.com. Cynthia also answers instructor-program questions from the Academy’s verified facts — see Talk to Cynthia.
